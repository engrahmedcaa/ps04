# Editorial policy (starter defaults; personalize before live collection)

- **Relevance:** accept only items that inform the central question for small professional practices; reject generic AI news without operational implications.
- **Freshness:** require a verifiable original publication date within the run's inclusive 30-day UTC window. Unknown or future dates are ineligible.
- **Credibility:** assess the evidence for each claim: methods, sample, provenance, primary documentation, and independent corroboration. Reputation and model confidence alone are insufficient.
- **Novelty:** distinguish a genuinely new finding from a repost, unchanged page with a refreshed date, or old event repackaged as news.
- **Perspective:** seek research, commercial, and practitioner evidence. Count publishers and underlying claims; mirrors or syndicated copies are not independent.
- **Uncertainty and conflict:** preserve disagreements, explain what could resolve them, and defer claims that cannot be responsibly represented.
- **Commercial incentives:** disclose vendor interests and treat announcements as claims until independently supported.
- **Exclusions:** promotional material with no inspectable evidence, duplicate episodes, undated content, unsupported causal claims, and material outside scope.

AI output is triage. A human reviewer must verify source passages, dates, independence, interpretations, and corrections before approval.
