# Brief creation instructions

Serve AI application developers at small teams. Use only accepted, eligible evidence and preserve exact version IDs and passage or transcript segment locators. Produce exactly three distinct briefs:

1. **What changed?** Identify recent agent tools/framework changes that matter to a small team and why.
2. **What holds up?** Examine readiness claims against methods, tasks, baselines, failure cases, version details, and independent evidence. Explain tensions without manufacturing disagreement.
3. **What should a small team do next?** Translate the evidence into a practical adoption or evaluation plan, including what to test before deployment.

Each argument must be supported by at least three independent publishers. Every factual or source claim needs a precise quote or media segment reference. Mark `reported_fact`, `source_claim`, and `inference` distinctly. For inferences, explain the chain of reasoning and what the synthesis adds. Discuss relevant limits, cost/maintenance uncertainty, security implications, and missing perspectives. Do not invent findings or advise production adoption when evidence is insufficient.
