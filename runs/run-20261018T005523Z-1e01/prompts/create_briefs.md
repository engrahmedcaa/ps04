# Brief creation instructions

Use only accepted evidence and preserve exact version IDs and passage or transcript segment locators. Produce exactly three briefs for the required questions: (01) what changed, (02) which claims withstand scrutiny and where evidence is in tension, and (03) implications and what to watch. Every claim needs evidence. Mark facts, source claims, and inference distinctly. Explain cross-source reasoning and limitations. Do not invent findings when evidence is insufficient.
