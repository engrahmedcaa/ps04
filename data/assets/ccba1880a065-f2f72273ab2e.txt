This is a meeting of the minds for anyone building AI agents — whether you’re a software engineer, AI/ML practitioner, data scientist, researcher, or engineering leader. Interrupt will take place on May 13-14 at The Midway in San Francisco. Space is limited to 750 attendees — grab your early-bird tickets here . A new era of AI agents pushing into production LangChain’s goal has always been to help developers build applications that can reason — and today, we’re seeing that accelerate with the movement to reimagine what’s possible using AI agents . More enterprises and startups are moving from prototype to production-ready LLM applications — and with that comes new challenges and points of discussion. As we push the boundaries of what agents can do – including how they can reason, act, and improve – Interrupt is a gathering place for the changemakers and builders who want to deep dive into AI agents. Learn best practices, explore breakthrough use cases, and meet the LangChain community The Interrupt in-person conference is broken into two action-packed days: May 13 : This is our pre-conference day with hands-on workshops by the LangChain team + a social hour to connect with fellow builders. May 14 : Be the first to experience new product features with our keynote, followed by fireside chats, expert talks, lightning demos, and plenty of good conversations. We’ll wrap up with an afterparty! At Interrupt, you’ll be able to: Learn from industry pioneers . Join thought leaders like Andrew Ng, Cognition Labs, Replit, 11x, and more as they share the latest innovations shaping the AI agent space. Get hands-on . May 13th is our pre-conference day, where we’ll have workshops focused on real-world, production-ready agent use cases. Get live instruction from the LangChain team and build up your AI agent toolkit alongside a group of peers. Explore how companies are using AI agents in production : Learn how different tools, techniques, and frameworks are helping companies ship impactful LLM apps to their users at scale across a range of verticals. Collaborate with fellow AI : Meet with fellow builders, engineers, and product-minded folks to exchange ideas that will propel the AI agent ecosystem forward. Stay tuned for our full agenda, which will be announced in the coming weeks. Don’t wait, register now Tickets are now available for Interrupt ! You can purchase an early-bird ticket for either the 1-day conference pass (May 14th) or the 2-day pass (May 13-14). We’re also looking for sponsors. Email us at sponsorships@langchain.dev to learn how to become a sponsor. Looking forward to seeing you all in May!
