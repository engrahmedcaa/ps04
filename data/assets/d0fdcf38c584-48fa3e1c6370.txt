People are moving beyond asking questions to using agents to carry out tasks from planning through completion. Our latest report from OpenAI Economic Research examines how agentic use is growing across enterprises and small businesses, focusing on differences in usage intensity and the kinds of work people do with ChatGPT. We have found that, among ChatGPT Enterprise customers, agentic use already represents a substantial share of activity ⁠ . In our EU sample, Codex and ChatGPT Work now account for more than half of output tokens. This analysis defines small or medium businesses as businesses with 200 or fewer associated users. The agentic shift extends to small and medium businesses. Since May 2026, weekly active users of Codex and ChatGPT Work among EU small and medium businesses grew more than eightfold, with growth across every member state. But we also found that the EU’s adoption of agentic AI is far from uniform. Among the ChatGPT Enterprise accounts in our sample, the share of output tokens generated through Codex and ChatGPT Work ranged from 22% in Slovenia to 85% in Lithuania. The differences across countries extends beyond the intensity of agentic use. The kinds of work people bring to ChatGPT also vary across countries. Frontier firms are defined as the top 10% of firms by output tokens per active user. Sample includes only ChatGPT Enterprise and Business accounts. The takeaway is that EU frontier firms use AI much more intensively than typical EU firms, but still trail firms at the global frontier. In September, frontier firms in the EU generated more than four times as many output tokens per active user as typical EU firms. Even so, frontier firms outside the EU generated more than twice as many output tokens per active user as frontier EU firms. EU-based firms also represented only 10% of the frontier firms globally, which is low considering that the EU accounts for a higher share of registered companies globally ⁠ (opens in a new window) . These figures reveal a substantial gap in how intensively businesses use AI. As agentic capabilities advance, the EU’s opportunity is to turn broader adoption into deeper use, stronger businesses, higher productivity and meaningful benefits for workers. Closing that gap means helping businesses move beyond experimentation, giving workers the skills and confidence to use AI, and making advanced capabilities more accessible and affordable. “Europe is now in a new era of AI, with tools that can carry out tasks for people. More than half of the AI use among our EU enterprise and business customers now involves these tools, and the number of small business users has grown 8x across the region. Yet there is still plenty of room for AI use to grow across the EU. Giving people the tools, skills and confidence to use AI well will help build stronger businesses and growing economies, with benefits felt across Europe.” Sarah Friar, Chief Financial Officer, OpenAI 2026 Economic Research Author OpenAI Keep reading View all Helping small businesses put AI to work Global Affairs Sep 30, 2026 OpenAI extends cyber access to Ukraine for civilian defense Global Affairs Sep 23, 2026 Sam Altman’s remarks at the United Nations Security Council Global Affairs Sep 23, 2026 Research Research Index Research Overview Economic Research Latest Advancements GPT-6.1 Sol GPT-6 Astra GPT-5.6 GPT-5.5 Safety Safety Approach Deployment Safety (opens in a new window) Security & Privacy Trust & Transparency Products ChatGPT (opens in a new window) ChatGPT Business (opens in a new window) ChatGPT Enterprise (opens in a new window) ChatGPT for Education (opens in a new window) Codex Dots (opens in a new window) Release Notes API Platform Overview API Log In (opens in a new window) Docs (opens in a new window) Business Overview Solutions Resources Plugins Customer Stories Partner Network Contact Sales Developers Apps SDK (opens in a new window) Open Models Docs (opens in a new window) Resources (opens in a new window) Developer Forum (opens in a new window) Company About Us Our Charter Careers News Support Help Center (opens in a new window) OpenAI Status (opens in a new window) More Stories Academy Supply Co.
