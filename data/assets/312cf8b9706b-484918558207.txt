As OpenAI builds more capable tools, we believe students will continue leading the way and bring others along with them. The OpenAI Student Collective gives undergraduate Campus Leads the tools, training, funding, and support to help that energy grow on campus. We’re looking for students excited to bring peers from every field together, help them gain confidence with OpenAI tools, and showcase what they create with a global community. How the program works Campus Leads work in pairs to create a place where students learn the latest AI tools, start projects, and share what they create along the way. Workshops Welcoming events where students learn new AI skills, explore what they can make, and choose a project to keep building during Studio Hours. Four per semester Studio Hours Weekly coworking sessions where students spend focused time on their projects, connect with peers, and get help when they need it. Weekly Showcases Campus-wide celebrations of what students are creating with AI. Once each semester Community A shared space for students to stay connected, ask questions, and discover upcoming opportunities. Seeing students proudly share the projects they built was a reminder of how powerful it is when students can see what’s possible—and then build it for themselves.” Arlina Yang, UC Davis Can I apply if I haven’t used Codex or built with AI before? Yes. You don’t need experience with Codex—or a portfolio of AI projects—to apply. We care more that you’re curious, eager to learn, and know how to bring people together. We’ll provide training and support to help you build confidence with new tools before introducing them on campus. Knowing what it feels like to be a beginner can also help you create a more welcoming experience for others who are just getting started. What happens after I apply? After reviewing applications, we'll invite selected applicants to interview in August so we can learn more about their motivation, initiative, and community mindset. Accepted Campus Leads will complete onboarding before launching campus programming in the fall. Who can I invite to participate in the community on my campus? Campus Leads will bring together students from across majors, experience levels, and campus communities. Events aren’t only for technical students or those already experimenting with AI—they should also feel welcoming and accessible to students who are just getting started. The goal is to create a space where anyone curious about AI can learn, experiment, and build alongside their peers. What is the overall time commitment for a Campus Lead? The program runs from onboarding in late August 2026 through June 2027, with campus programming beginning in September. Campus Leads should expect to spend an average of 4–6 hours per week hosting events, participating in training and check-ins, and supporting their campus community. Some weeks will be lighter, while workshops and showcases may require more time. Will OpenAI provide workshop materials or do I need to create everything myself? You won’t need to create everything from scratch. OpenAI will provide workshop guides, templates, training, and resources to help you plan and run sessions on your campus. Every student community is different, so you’ll have room to adapt these materials and shape workshops around what your peers are most excited to learn or build. How long will the workshops be? Most workshops should last one to two hours, though the format may vary depending on the topic and audience. We’ll provide flexible workshop guides that you can adapt to what students on your campus want to learn or build. Do I need to start an official club to become a Campus Lead? No. You can apply and begin hosting events without establishing an official campus club. We’ll provide resources, programming ideas, funding, and support to help you bring students together from the start. If official recognition would make it easier to book rooms, reach students, or build credibility on your campus, we can also support you through that process. Can I request a specific co-lead? We know that campus programming is most successful when two people can lean on each other and build together. We review every application closely and take care to pair each lead with a complementary co-lead. If you have a friend or classmate who you think complements your skill set and helps reach a different part of campus, we encourage you to each apply on your own and mention one another as part of your application. Can I participate in another ambassador program while being a Campus Lead? Campus Leads cannot participate in another ambassador program while serving in this role. You’re still welcome to participate in campus clubs, student organizations, research groups, internships, and other leadership roles, as long as they don’t create a conflict or prevent you from meeting the program commitment. Can postgraduate students apply? The Campus Lead role is currently open to undergraduate students who expect to graduate after December 2027. Postgraduate students are still welcome to attend campus events and workshops. How will I receive my stipend and when? Campus Leads will be paid through our secure payment platform. We’ll share details during onboarding, including payment timing, payment method, and any required documentation. Stipends will be paid at the end of each semester, after Campus Leads have participated in the program and completed the expected semester activities.
