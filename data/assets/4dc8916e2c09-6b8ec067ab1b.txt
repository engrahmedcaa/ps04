How to evaluate GPT‑5 for your business Subject-matter expertise Run tasks across multiple functions-e.g., drafting legal text, producing market analysis, debugging a codebase-then compare GPT‑5's output quality, completeness, and time saved against human benchmarks. Reliable and fast responses Give GPT‑5 fact-based and multi-step tasks; check factual accuracy, citation quality, and how well it handles clarifications. Measure average response time across low, medium, and high complexity prompts. Advanced reasoning built in Give the model a complex, ambiguous problem that requires multi-step reasoning with minimal guidance. Assess its solution quality, relevance of follow-up questions, and real-world application. Understands your company context Upload multiple internal files or connect your company's apps (e.g., Google Drive, SharePoint, GitHub), then evaluate how well GPT‑5 synthesizes answers using your latest content, internal terminology, and guidelines.
